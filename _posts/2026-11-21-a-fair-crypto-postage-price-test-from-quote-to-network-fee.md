## Keep the test reproducible

- **Lock shipment inputs.** Record packed weight, units, outside dimensions, packaging, full addresses, mailing date and requested options. Use consistent rounding. For international labels, keep customs details identical too.
- **Match the service.** Compare the exact carrier service, not similar delivery estimates. If a service or option differs, mark the pairing **not directly comparable**.
- **Capture fresh quotes.** Request them close together on the same day. Save timestamps, time zones, crypto amounts and displayed payment windows. Refresh expired quotes.
- **Control payment variables.** Use the same cryptocurrency and funding source where possible. Report different currencies or funding methods as separate scenarios.
- **Label the evidence.** Quotes alone establish **quoted cost**, not **paid cost**. Don’t buy duplicate labels just to complete a comparison.

For direct payment, calculate **invoice value + separately charged payment fees**. Keep original crypto amounts and convert components into one reporting currency using a documented exchange-rate source and timestamp.

Record actual network fees, separate exchange withdrawal charges and identifiable conversion costs. Don’t count a network fee twice if it’s included in a withdrawal charge. Record unclear breakdowns as limitations.
