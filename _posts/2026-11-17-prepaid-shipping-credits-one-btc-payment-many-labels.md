## Keep funding and shipment records separate

Treat your local ledger as two linked record sets. These are suggested bookkeeping fields, not documented API fields or CSV columns.

- **Funding record:** internal reference, payment date, asset, amount sent, transaction ID, network fee and credits added.
- **Label record:** store order reference, purchase date, carrier, service, tracking number, credits spent and refund status.
- **Reconciliation:** compare local records with account label history, and keep each label document with its shipment record.

The network fee goes to the blockchain, not the label seller. Prepaying doesn't remove postage charges. It spreads the funding transaction's network cost across labels purchased with those credits.

If you allocate that fee equally, divide it by the number of labels funded. That's a bookkeeping convention, not a guaranteed saving. Leave part unallocated if the corresponding credits remain unspent.

Before funding, confirm destinations, packed weights, dimensions and actual service prices. Checkout supplies a unique address and exact crypto amount. Check that any exchange withdrawal deduction won't reduce the amount received, then save the payment reference and recovery link. Don't make urgent dispatch depend on a last-minute Bitcoin transaction.
