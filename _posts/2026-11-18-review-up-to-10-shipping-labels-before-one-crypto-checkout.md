Treat checkout as a commit point: review each package before sending payment. [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com) offers Multi-Ship for up to 10 shipments in one checkout, but each bitcoin postage shipment still needs its own acceptance check.

Start with a local worksheet containing one entry per physical package. It’s a review artifact, not a required upload format or integration schema. Give similar boxes temporary identifiers so you can keep recipients, shipment entries, and printed labels matched.

## Pre-payment checklist

- **Address:** Compare recipient details with the original order. Check apartment or suite numbers separately. Verify the From address, especially for pickup.
- **Package:** Measure the packed parcel, including its box and cushioning. Check weight units, carrier limits, and the selected service’s requirements.
- **Service:** Read the service name for every entry. A shared carrier doesn’t mean every package needs the same service.
- **Customs:** For international packages, verify contents description, value, quantity, and origin. The receiver may still owe import taxes depending on the service.
- **Batch:** Confirm there are no more than 10 shipments, every intended package has an entry, and no order appears twice. Recheck exceptions before approving payment.

Carrier pickup can be requested during checkout and is booked after payment using the shipment’s From address.

If your orders start in a spreadsheet, Bulk CSV is another available workflow. WooCommerce store sync and a developer API are separate tools; [learn more at Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com) before choosing your workflow.

At payment, use the unique address and exact crypto amount displayed. Check withdrawal fees: a fee deducted from the amount sent can leave the invoice short-paid. Keep your order ID and the recovery link beneath the payment QR code before leaving checkout.

Once labels are available, reconcile each one with its recipient and physical package before printing or attaching it.
