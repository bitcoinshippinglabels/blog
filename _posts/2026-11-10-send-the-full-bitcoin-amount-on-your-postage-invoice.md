A btc postage invoice defines the amount that must reach the payment address—not necessarily the amount removed from your exchange balance. At [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com), checkout provides a unique address and an exact crypto amount, with payments processed through NOWPayments.

Treat the final transaction preview as a deployment checkpoint. The condition to verify is simple: **recipient amount = invoice amount**. Your total balance debit may be higher because of fees.

An exchange withdrawal fee can be deducted from the amount you enter or charged separately. A Bitcoin network fee pays for blockchain transaction processing; it doesn’t go to the label seller. Self-custody wallets may also offer fee subtraction, so check the preview rather than trusting a field labeled “Amount.”

- **Read the active invoice.** Copy its address and exact BTC amount; don’t reuse an earlier order’s details.
- **Match asset, network and units.** Follow checkout instructions and distinguish BTC from satoshis or fiat estimates.
- **Inspect the recipient amount.** Account for withdrawal deductions. If the interface can’t send the required amount exactly, use another funding method rather than rounding down.
- **Budget for fees.** A balance equal to the invoice may be insufficient.
- **Verify and retain.** Compare the pasted address with checkout, then save the order ID and transaction ID until you have the label.

For payment records, integer satoshis avoid binary floating-point rounding errors. Preserve the invoice’s exact value instead of rebuilding it from a rounded fiat estimate. This is general accounting practice, not an API specification.

Track amount and payment progress separately. A submitted withdrawal doesn’t establish receipt, and waiting won’t repair a short payment. If you’ve underpaid, gather the invoice amount, delivered amount, IDs and fee details, then ask support how to proceed before sending again.

For repeat purchases, an optional funded account supports instant label purchases from its balance; [learn more at Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com). Funding still needs the same fee checks.
