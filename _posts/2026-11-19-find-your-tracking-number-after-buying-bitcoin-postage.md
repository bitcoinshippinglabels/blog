After buying bitcoin postage, track two separate objects: the cryptocurrency payment and the physical shipment. At [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com), labels are generated after payment is detected, usually within minutes. A blockchain transaction ID helps investigate the transfer; the carrier tracking number follows the parcel.

## Locate, validate, store

- **Locate:** Open the generated label. The carrier tracking identifier is typically near a barcode, but placement varies by carrier and service. For USPS ZPL labels, inspect the rendered or printed version.
- **Validate:** Don’t copy a routing code, postal code, or service identifier by mistake. Preserve letters and leading zeros, and check for accidental spaces.
- **Separate:** Use the order ID for order recovery, the transaction ID in the relevant blockchain explorer, and the tracking number in the selected carrier’s tracking service.
- **Store:** Save the carrier, tracking number, store order reference, and handoff date together. Keep the payment transaction ID in a separate field.

Closed checkout? Recover the existing order before buying another label. There’s a recovery link under the payment QR code, and the Recover Order page accepts an order ID or email. Recovery doesn’t require an account; [learn more at Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com).

If the label hasn’t appeared, check payment first. Verify the checkout address, the amount received, and blockchain progress. An exchange withdrawal fee can leave the invoice short-paid. Keep the order ID and transaction details ready for support.

Once you have the tracking number, open the official tracking tool for the carrier printed on the label. A “label created” event can appear before physical handoff. An acceptance or pickup event is stronger evidence that the carrier received the parcel. A booked pickup isn’t a completed pickup.

If tracking stalls after handoff, recheck the number, retain your receipt, and read the latest event and timestamp. Scan gaps alone don’t establish loss. Contact the carrier for a physical-shipment investigation.

Open your saved label now, copy its tracking number into the correct carrier’s tracking tool, and record the latest event beside your store order.
