A btc postage checkout has two separate concerns: the label purchase and the crypto transfer. [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com) processes crypto payments through NOWPayments, with a unique payment address and an exact amount shown at checkout.

Small price movements during the payment window are tolerated, but the payment still needs to cover the price. There’s no published percentage or exact window duration to build into your workflow. Send the full displayed amount promptly, after checking the transfer details.

**Pre-send checklist**

- **Prepare funds:** Have your wallet ready and enough funds for the payment plus applicable sending costs. Inspect an exchange’s withdrawal process before opening checkout.
- **Match checkout:** Check the selected cryptocurrency, copy the displayed address, and verify the destination in your wallet.
- **Check net delivery:** Use the exact requested amount, without rounding or making your own conversion. Confirm that the recipient will receive that amount after fees.
- **Save recovery details:** Keep the recovery link beneath the payment QR code. Save the transaction reference alongside your order ID.
- **Authorize promptly:** Once the checks pass, send rather than leaving checkout idle during unrelated work.

The fee check matters. An exchange can deduct its withdrawal fee from the amount you enter, leaving the invoice short. A wallet that charges its sending fee separately instead requires enough funds for both. Price tolerance doesn’t cover a fee deduction.

Keep authorization and detection separate. [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com) generates the label after payment is detected, usually within minutes; a busy Bitcoin network can take longer. If progress is unclear, inspect the transfer record and recipient amount, then recover the existing order before sending again. Contact support if the payment and order state remain unclear.
