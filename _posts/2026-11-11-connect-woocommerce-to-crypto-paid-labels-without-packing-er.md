Connecting WooCommerce to bitcoin postage should replace repeated order entry with review, while keeping packing checks intact. [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com) offers WooCommerce store sync, but verify the current integration instructions before assuming how authentication, sync timing or tracking writeback works.

Start with a controlled order whose address and contents you know. Treat the test as an acceptance gate, not permission to automate every action.

- **Eligibility:** Establish which orders reach the shipping workflow and how staff exclude unpaid, cancelled or already handled orders.
- **Transfer:** Compare recipient and shipment details against WooCommerce before checkout. Record missing fields and manual steps.
- **Correction:** Change an order detail and check how that edit reaches the shipping workflow.
- **Retry:** Reopen the order and establish how an operator identifies an existing label purchase. Don’t buy extra labels just to inspect screen behavior.
- **Tracking:** Verify whether tracking returns to WooCommerce. If it doesn’t, assign someone to enter it.

Keep the store order reference and shipping purchase reference together. They aren’t interchangeable. Give each test shipment one owner, with a visible handoff rule for shared queues.

The packed parcel remains the measurement source. Match its items to the order, review recipient and From addresses, then weigh and measure the finished box. Packaging adds weight. Review the selected carrier service and shipment price before paying.

Keep payment handling separate from sync. At [Bitcoin Shipping Labels](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com), checkout shows a unique payment address and exact crypto amount. Send the full amount shown; an exchange withdrawal fee deducted from the transfer can leave the invoice short-paid.

Track “order selected,” “payment submitted” and “label generated” as separate states. Payment submission alone doesn’t mean there’s a printable label. If a tab closes, check the existing purchase through recovery before retrying.
