The label is printed. Then you spot it: the apartment number is missing. Buying bitcoin postage does not change that basic shipping problem—small address mistakes can turn a quick dispatch into a support request.

## Start with the recipient, not the printer

Check the street number, apartment or suite, city and postal code against the recipient's details. A suggested address is useful, but it is not a reason to drop information the recipient supplied.

Next, measure and weigh the packed box. Not the empty box. Not the item before you added padding. Use those packed measurements when you [calculate shipping costs](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com/calculator).

## Read the label once before handing it over

Check that the recipient and service match what you intended to buy. Keep the barcode clear and avoid wrapping it around an edge. Remove old labels from reused packaging so there is no competing destination on the box.

For carrier-specific instructions, use the [USPS Click-N-Ship guide](https://www.usps.com/ship/online-shipping.htm). Payment method and packing requirements are separate questions; paying with cryptocurrency does not replace the carrier's acceptance rules.

Got a question about the platform? The [Bitcoin Shipping Labels FAQ](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com/faq) is a better starting point than guessing at checkout.

Once the details are right, [create a shipping label with Bitcoin](https://www.bitcoinshippinglabels.com/shipping). Read it, print it, and give that address one last look.
